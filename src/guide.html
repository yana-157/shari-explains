For Shari

Your next post,
in a few easy steps.

1. Open your writing desk.

In the repository folder, run npm run studio. Open the Post Studio link it prints. Keep Terminal running while you write.

2. Add your story.

Click New post. Add a headline, a short introduction, category, date, and cover image. Paste your text into the article box. Leave a blank line between paragraphs. The live preview handles the formatting.

3. Save it.

Click Save post. Your article and image go straight into their own folder in the repository. The local website updates automatically. There is nothing to download or move.

4. Publish when you’re ready.

Click Publish to live site. The Studio commits that post to GitHub, which rebuilds the website. A draft can stay on your computer until you choose to publish.

Need to make a correction?

Choose the article in Edit a post, make your changes, save, and publish again. Keep the article address unchanged so readers’ bookmarks still work.
